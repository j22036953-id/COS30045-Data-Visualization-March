📋 Data Storyboard – Planning & Narrative Flow

Audience: Cost‑conscious Australian families & students | Goal: Guide smart, energy‑efficient TV buying

STEP 1

Market landscape: Which screen technologies are most common?

❓ "What are my options?"
📊 Bar chart | X: Screen technology | Y: Count of models

Insight: LCD (LED) dominates (>900 models).

Why bar chart: Best for comparing categories.

STEP 2

Screen size availability

❓ "What sizes can I buy?"
📊 Bar chart | X: Size (inches) | Y: Number of models

Insight: Wide range, peaks at 50",55",65".

Why bar chart: Shows frequency distribution.

STEP 3

Brand variety: Who offers the most models?

❓ "Which brands should I consider?"
📊 Bar chart | X: Brand | Y: Distinct model count

Insight: Samsung, LG, Diviox lead.

Why bar chart: Easy to see top brands.

STEP 4

Power consumption by technology

❓ "Which technology saves me the most?"
📊 Bar chart | X: Technology | Y: Avg power (Watts)

Insight: Standard LCD = 87W (most efficient).

Why bar chart: Direct comparison of averages.

STEP 5

Relationship: Screen size vs. Power consumption

❓ "Does a bigger screen cost more to run?"
📈 Scatter plot | X: Screen size | Y: Power (Watts)

Insight: Strong positive correlation.

Why scatter plot: Shows relationship between two continuous variables.

STEP 6

Star rating vs. Screen size – does it help?

❓ "Can I trust the star rating?"
📈 Scatter plot | X: Screen size | Y: Star rating

Insight: Weak/no relationship – stars alone are misleading.

Why scatter plot: Shows lack of correlation.
